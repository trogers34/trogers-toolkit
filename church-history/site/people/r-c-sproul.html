R. C. Sproul

1939–2017 · Modern

Also known as Robert Charles Sproul

Presbyterian theologian, teacher, and founder of Ligonier Ministries

American Reformed theologian who made classic Calvinist theology accessible to lay audiences through teaching, books, radio, and Ligonier Ministries, and who became a leading defender of biblical inerrancy and justification by faith alone.

Born
13 February 1939 · Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, United States[4]
Died
14 December 2017, aged 78 · Altamonte Springs, Florida, United States[4]

What they did

  • Founded Ligonier Ministries (1971), which spread Reformed theology to a wide lay audience through teaching series, conferences, Tabletalk magazine, and the radio program Renewing Your Mind.[4]
  • Wrote more than 100 books, including The Holiness of God (1985), his best-known work, and Chosen by God (1986), a defense of the Reformed doctrine of predestination.[1][2][4]
  • Was a leader of the International Council on Biblical Inerrancy and helped produce the Chicago Statement on Biblical Inerrancy (1978).[3][4]
  • Defended justification by faith alone against the 1994 Evangelicals and Catholics Together statement, in books such as Faith Alone (1995).[4]
  • Served as general editor of the New Geneva Study Bible (1995), later the Reformation Study Bible.[4]
  • Taught philosophy and theology at colleges and seminaries, and later founded Saint Andrew's Chapel (1997) and Reformation Bible College (2011) in Florida.[4]

Persecution, arrests, and executions

R. C. Sproul was never arrested, imprisoned, or persecuted.

Excommunications

R. C. Sproul was never excommunicated or disciplined by any church. He was ordained in the mainline United Presbyterian Church and later served as a teaching elder in the Presbyterian Church in America.
