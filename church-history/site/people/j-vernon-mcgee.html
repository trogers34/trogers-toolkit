J. Vernon McGee

1904–1988 · Modern

Also known as John Vernon McGee

Presbyterian pastor and radio Bible teacher; host of Thru the Bible

A Texas-born Presbyterian minister who pastored the Church of the Open Door in Los Angeles and became one of the best-known radio Bible teachers in the world. His Thru the Bible programme took listeners through the whole Bible in five years, in a folksy Southern style, and has been translated and broadcast in more than a hundred languages since his death.

Died

What they did

  • Created Thru the Bible, a radio course that covered the entire Bible in five years of daily half-hour programmes; it was rebroadcast after his death and translated into more than a hundred languages.[1][2]
  • Pastored the Church of the Open Door in Los Angeles, a large independent fundamentalist church linked with the Bible Institute of Los Angeles (Biola), for over twenty years.[2]
  • Taught a plain, verse-by-verse style with homespun humour and a strong Texas accent, aimed at ordinary listeners rather than scholars.[1][2]
  • Held dispensational, premillennial views typical of Dallas Theological Seminary, which shaped his teaching on prophecy and Israel.[1]
  • His radio talks were published as a five-volume Thru the Bible commentary (1981–1983).[1]
  • Grew up poor; his father was killed in an accident when he was a teenager, and he worked to pay for his education.[2]
